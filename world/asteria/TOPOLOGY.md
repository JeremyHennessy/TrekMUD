# USS Asteria topology — v1 candidate

USS Asteria is TrekMUD's original Nebula-class player ship.

## Canon/reference constraints

- Nebula-class starship in service during the TNG/DS9 era.
- Approximately 442 m long in official reference material.
- Configurable dorsal mission pod.
- Multi-mission profile with strong scientific and sensor capability.
- Large technical-reference crew complement.

## TrekMUD setting fill

The exact interior is original campaign geography, not a copied fan deck plan.

- **28 numbered decks**
- **4-level triangular science/sensor pod**
- **200 persistent locations**
- **206 explicit graph connections**
- central turbolift + corridor hub on every numbered deck
- stable room IDs prefixed `AST-`
- all locations reachable from the opening transporter room

The 28-deck allocation is a TrekMUD technical/layout choice rather than an on-screen canon claim.
