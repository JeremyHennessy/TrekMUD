# USS Meridian crew model — candidate baseline

Status: **CANDIDATE / UNAPPROVED**.

The Meridian has a nominal complement of **500**. TrekMUD does not pretend to continuously simulate 500 fully realized people.

## Three persistence tiers

### Tier 1 — core persistent NPCs

Senior officers and people who become personally important. Their public service facts are stored in the shared repository. Personality, private goals, secrets and hidden relationship state belong in private GM state once private storage exists.

### Tier 2 — persistent named crew

Stable identity, species, rank, department, billet, shift and other ordinary directory facts. They become richer only if play gives them more importance.

### Tier 3 — background population

The remaining complement exists statistically until an interaction makes a specific identity useful.

If the player unexpectedly asks for "the technician who serviced this relay yesterday", the GM may materialize a plausible Tier-3 person. Once materialized, that person gets a stable ID and is never silently regenerated later.

These totals are simulation scaffolding, not a claim that canon publishes this exact Akira-class staffing table.

## Duty rotation

The Meridian uses a normal three-shift rotation:

- Alpha
- Beta
- Gamma

The player's eventual shift is **not predetermined**. It depends on department, billet, onboarding and later career changes.

## Opening continuity

The directory deliberately preserves two people already implied by the proposed opening scene:

- **Chief Petty Officer Lian Okafor**, Transporter Chief in Transporter Room 2
- **Crewman Brel Joras**, the young Bolian arriving on the neighboring pad

This converts those previously generic figures into persistent candidate identities rather than replacing them later.

## Public vs hidden information

The repository is currently public.

Therefore the shared crew directory contains only information the player's character could plausibly obtain from an ordinary Starfleet directory or from direct observation. It does **not** contain:

- secret motives
- hidden loyalties
- private opinions of the player
- planned betrayals
- romance flags
- unrevealed personal history
- plot outcomes

Those require private GM state before the campaign begins.
