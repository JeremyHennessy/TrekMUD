# Skill and career advancement — proposed v1.0

Status: **proposal**.

## No XP total

TrekMUD has no universal experience-point currency and no visible "points until promotion" meter.

Advancement follows demonstrated history.

## Skill growth

Skill ranks require evidence appropriate to the new level.

### 0 → 1: Trained

Normally requires formal instruction, supervised practice, Academy/extension coursework, or repeated successful field use.

### 1 → 2: Proficient

Requires sustained independent use, a pattern of competent performance and enough experience that the skill is part of the officer's normal professional toolkit.

### 2 → 3: Expert

Requires repeated success on genuinely difficult work, recognized expertise, advanced training or mentorship, and evidence across more than one isolated incident.

### 3 → 4: Exceptional

Rare. Requires a sustained career-level record establishing the character as unusually capable even among experienced Starfleet professionals.

No rank increase occurs simply because a fixed number of checks were rolled.

## Promotion readiness

Promotion is a Starfleet personnel decision, not a level-up.

A promotion review considers:

- plausible time in grade
- mastery of current responsibilities
- required qualifications
- sustained performance pattern
- conduct and disciplinary record
- leadership appropriate to the next rank
- recommendation/confidence of superior officers
- organizational need and available opportunities

A character can be highly skilled and remain at the same rank. A promotion can occur without an immediate billet change.

## Billets

Rank and billet are independent.

Temporary acting assignments are recorded explicitly and do not automatically grant permanent rank or position.

Billet progression can include specialist and command routes. The campaign does not assume the player must eventually become captain.

## Evaluations

Formal or informal performance reviews should occur at believable career intervals and after unusually important assignments.

Evaluations summarize evidence already created by play; they should not invent a promotion justification retroactively.

## Transfers

Transfer opportunities may appear because of:

- career development
- personal request
- department need
- promotion
- disciplinary reassignment
- specialist demand
- major fleet events

The player may decline opportunities when Starfleet circumstances plausibly allow it.
