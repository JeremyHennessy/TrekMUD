# Organic Character Discovery — Rules v1.1

Status: **APPROVED — 2026-10-06**

This revision replaces pre-play character-sheet completion with **discovery through play**.

## Required starting facts

Before revision 1, establish only:

- name;
- species;
- age;
- pronouns if desired;
- homeworld / place of upbringing;
- Starfleet department.

Everything else may emerge later.

## Mechanical budget is preserved

Rules v1.1 does not grant more or fewer starting mechanical resources than v1.0.

Eventually the new Ensign still has:

- attributes `2,2,1,1,1`;
- primary department skill rank 2;
- three additional broad skills at rank 1;
- one department specialty;
- one open secondary specialty.

The difference is **when those choices are locked**.

## Unresolved is not zero

An unresolved attribute or skill is not treated as 0.

It means the campaign has not yet established which legal starting value belongs there.

Until resolution is necessary, leave it unresolved.

## Discovery lock

A detail locks when:

1. the player explicitly establishes it;
2. repeated portrayal establishes it and the player accepts the interpretation; or
3. the value becomes mechanically necessary.

For a mechanically necessary lock:

- establish the value before rolling;
- use prior portrayal/background evidence when available;
- otherwise the player chooses from the remaining legal pool;
- persist the choice;
- then roll;
- never change the locked value because the result was unfavorable.

## Attributes

All five attributes begin unresolved.

The remaining attribute pool begins:

`[2, 2, 1, 1, 1]`

Locking an attribute removes one matching value from the pool.

When the pool has one value left, the final unresolved attribute inherits it automatically.

## Broad skills

The department choice immediately establishes the corresponding professional skill at rank 2.

Three **rank-1 Academy training slots** remain unresolved.

When play establishes prior training in another broad skill, one rank-1 slot may be consumed.

Unresolved broad skills are neither rank 0 nor rank 1 until needed.

If a check requires an unresolved broad skill and the player does not establish it as one of their remaining Academy-trained skills, it resolves as rank 0 for the starting profile and becomes locked at 0.

## Specialties

Two specialty slots remain unresolved:

- one specialty must belong to the primary department skill;
- one secondary specialty may belong to any broad skill the character has legitimately established.

A specialty should normally emerge from actual interests, training or repeated behavior rather than optimization for a single roll.

## Background and personality

Background is an expanding fact ledger, not a mandatory prewritten biography.

Facts about family, Academy history, hobbies, prior friendships, personal habits, ambitions and cultural background may be established when they naturally become relevant.

Once a fact materially affects play, it becomes persistent campaign state.

## Species traits

Species does not assign personality.

Canon biological/cultural facts may apply when appropriate, but individual traits should be explicitly established rather than inferred stereotypically.

## Career ambition

The player is not required to choose a long-term career endpoint.

Wanting command, avoiding command, changing departments, becoming a specialist, transferring, or leaving Starfleet can emerge through the campaign.

## GM constraint

The GM may **propose** a discovery based on established portrayal but may not secretly assign unresolved traits to create a desired outcome.

The player retains final authority over legal unresolved starting choices until they are locked.

## Persistence

Every locked discovery should record:

- what was established;
- how it was established;
- campaign revision/stardate;
- whether it consumed a starting mechanical slot.

Once locked and used in play, it cannot be retconned merely for optimization. Ordinary narrative corrections remain possible through the campaign's explicit correction/version process.
