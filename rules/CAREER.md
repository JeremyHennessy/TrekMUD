# Starfleet Career Model — draft v0.9

## Rank progression

Initial commissioned-officer path:

1. Ensign
2. Lieutenant junior grade
3. Lieutenant
4. Lieutenant Commander
5. Commander
6. Captain

Higher flag ranks may exist in the setting but are outside the initial progression target.

Promotion is not level-up XP. It considers time in service, performance, qualifications, conduct, leadership, recommendations and available billets.

## Billet is separate from rank

Examples:

- Ensign — Junior Engineering Officer
- Ensign — Damage Control Specialist
- Lieutenant (j.g.) — Assistant Chief Engineer
- Lieutenant — Engineering Watch Officer
- Lieutenant — Chief Propulsion Specialist
- Lieutenant Commander — Chief Engineer
- Commander — Executive Officer

A promotion does not automatically change the billet, and temporary acting responsibility does not automatically change rank.

## Starting specialty families

- Engineering — warp systems, propulsion, power, damage control, computer systems
- Operations — ship operations, resource allocation, transporters, mission operations
- Science — astrophysics, stellar cartography, xenobiology, geology, sensor sciences
- Tactical — weapons, defensive systems, tactical analysis
- Security — investigations, shipboard security, away-team security
- Flight Control — helm, navigation, shuttlecraft
- Medical — medicine, surgery, emergency medicine, exobiology
- Counseling — psychology, crew welfare, diplomatic support

The player chooses one primary and one secondary specialty during character creation.

## Skill growth

Skills improve from use, study, mentorship, training, holodeck simulations, extension courses and meaningful success/failure. Advancement should reflect what the character actually spends time doing.
