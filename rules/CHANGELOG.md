# TrekMUD Rules Changelog

## v1.0 — 2026-10-06

Status: **APPROVED BASELINE**

Initial campaign rules baseline.

Locked concepts include:

- open-ended, non-railroad campaign play;
- persistent world/NPC continuity;
- competence-first resolution;
- 2d6 + Attribute + Skill + Specialty + situation;
- targets 7 / 9 / 11 / 13 / 15;
- no automatic critical successes or fumbles;
- no repeated-roll fishing without changed circumstances;
- social checks cannot erase NPC agency;
- evidence-based skill/career advancement rather than XP;
- rank and billet as separate concepts;
- injury/fatigue states rather than hit points;
- persistent relationships and consequences;
- deterministic auditable SHA256_COUNTER_V1 randomness;
- repository checkpoints as the continuity authority.

The exact immutable baseline is identified by the Git tag `rules-v1.0` applied after merge.
