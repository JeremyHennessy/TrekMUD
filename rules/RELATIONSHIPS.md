# Relationships and NPC continuity — v1.0

Status: **APPROVED BASELINE — 2026-10-06**.

## Relationships are not a persuasion score

NPCs are persistent people with their own careers, preferences, loyalties and relationships.

There is no universal "friendship XP" or guaranteed romance path.

## Player-visible relationship state

The public campaign state records only information the player character can reasonably know, such as:

- relationship type
- shared history
- observed level of familiarity
- promises or obligations
- known conflicts
- the character's current impression of the relationship

Objective hidden feelings, secret motives and private loyalties belong in GM-only state once a private storage location exists.

## Persistence

Important interactions can change later behavior.

Examples:

- repeatedly helping a colleague may create trust;
- taking credit for someone's work may damage professional respect;
- a private confidence may create intimacy or later conflict;
- an argument may cool down with time rather than disappearing after one scene.

NPCs also form and change relationships with one another.

## Materialization

Background crew become persistent named people when interaction makes their identity matter.

Once materialized, the same person remains the same person across sessions: species, department, rank, history and relevant traits are not regenerated for convenience.
