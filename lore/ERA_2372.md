# 2372 Era Anchor — draft

Status: **research anchor, not yet an approved campaign checkpoint**.

TrekMUD currently proposes a campaign start at stardate **49317.4** in **2372**. The goal is the political and cultural atmosphere of late *The Next Generation* / mid-*Deep Space Nine*, not a replay of television episodes.

## What is already true

By 2372:

- The USS Enterprise-D era has ended, while the people and institutions established during *The Next Generation* remain part of contemporary Starfleet.
- Deep Space 9 is an important Federation/Bajoran strategic point at the Bajoran wormhole.
- The Dominion is a known strategic threat.
- Klingon-Federation relations have sharply deteriorated during the events surrounding the Klingon invasion of Cardassia.
- Worf has joined Deep Space 9.
- Changeling infiltration is a serious Starfleet security concern.
- USS Voyager is missing far from normal Federation contact; ordinary Alpha/Beta Quadrant personnel do not have omniscient knowledge of its Delta Quadrant experiences.

## Stardate caution

Star Trek stardates are useful continuity anchors but are not perfectly monotonic with episode production/release order.

Memory Alpha's timeline data places:

- DS9 "The Way of the Warrior" at 49011.4
- DS9 "Starship Down" at 49263.5
- DS9 "The Sword of Kahless" at 49289.1
- DS9 "Our Man Bashir" at 49300.7
- DS9 "Sons of Mogh" at 49556.2
- DS9 "Rules of Engagement" at 49665.3
- DS9 "To the Death" at 49904.2
- DS9 "Broken Link" at 49962.4

"Homefront" is an example of why episode order cannot be inferred from a single stardate alone: its dating spans/uses values that overlap this portion of the chronology.

Therefore **49317.4 is a campaign anchor, not permission to infer an exact unseen episode ordering**. Canon events become active only when their chronology is supported well enough for the simulation.
