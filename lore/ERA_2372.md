# 2372 Era Anchor

Status: **APPROVED SETTING BASELINE**

TrekMUD begins in **2372**, stardate **49317.4**, during the overlap of late *The Next Generation* influence and *Deep Space Nine*'s escalating Dominion-era politics.

The goal is to inhabit this historical moment, not reenact television episodes.

## Already true by the campaign start

At the campaign's opening point:

- the Enterprise-D era has ended, but the institutions, technology and people established during *The Next Generation* are contemporary Starfleet;
- Deep Space 9 is a major Federation/Bajoran strategic point at the Bajoran wormhole;
- the Dominion is a known strategic threat;
- Klingon-Federation relations have deteriorated sharply around the Klingon invasion of Cardassia;
- Worf is serving on Deep Space 9;
- Changeling infiltration is a serious Starfleet security concern;
- USS Voyager is missing and ordinary Alpha/Beta Quadrant personnel do not possess omniscient knowledge of its Delta Quadrant experiences.

## Chronology caution

Star Trek stardates are useful anchors but are not perfectly monotonic with production or broadcast order.

The campaign therefore does **not** infer unseen events merely because one episode has a nearby stardate.

A later canon fact can exist in the repository while remaining:

- not yet true;
- not yet known to Starfleet;
- not yet known to the player.

## Knowledge-gate rule

Every lore fact used during play is conceptually assigned four questions:

- **Is it canonically true?**
- **Is it true yet at the current campaign date?**
- **Could Starfleet reasonably know it yet?**
- **Does the player character know it?**

Only the final layer is presented as direct character knowledge.

## Canon divergence

TrekMUD's USS Asteria, crew and missions are original setting material living alongside canon.

Player action may create local divergence. Major canon is not casually overwritten, but if the player becomes involved in circumstances where a real divergence is plausible, that divergence is recorded explicitly rather than secretly steering the story back to television continuity.

## Tone target

The setting should feel like everyday Starfleet life in the middle 2370s:

- high professional competence;
- exploration and scientific work;
- diplomacy and Federation bureaucracy;
- routine maintenance and shipboard social life;
- increasing security pressure around the Dominion;
- occasional large-scale events without making the player the center of galactic history.
