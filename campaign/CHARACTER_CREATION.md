# TrekMUD character creation — organic v1.1

Character creation is intentionally minimal.

The player begins as a newly assigned **Starfleet Ensign** aboard USS Meridian. The campaign discovers most of the character through play rather than requiring a complete character sheet before Scene One.

## Required before r00001

Only these facts are required:

1. **Name**
2. **Species**
3. **Age**
4. **Pronouns** — optional
5. **Homeworld / where the character grew up**
6. **Starfleet department**

Normal starting departments:

- Engineering
- Operations
- Science
- Tactical
- Security
- Flight Control
- Medical
- Counseling

A different Starfleet path can be proposed if it is plausible in 2372.

## What is deliberately unresolved

At r00001, the following remain open unless the player has already volunteered them naturally:

- attribute placement;
- secondary/cross-training skills;
- primary specialty;
- secondary specialty;
- hobbies and interests;
- strengths outside the department;
- weaknesses/development areas;
- detailed Academy history;
- family;
- friendships before the Meridian;
- cultural habits;
- personal history;
- long-term career ambition;
- most individual species/background traits.

These are **not missing data**. They are intentional discovery space.

## Mechanical discovery pools

Rules v1.1 preserves the same eventual starting mechanical budget as v1.0:

- attribute pool: `2, 2, 1, 1, 1`;
- primary department skill: rank **2**, established immediately by department;
- Academy training pool: **three rank-1 broad-skill slots**;
- specialty pool: **one department specialty + one open secondary specialty**.

Unresolved values are represented as `null` plus the remaining pool. They are never silently treated as zero.

## Discovery lock rule

A currently unresolved detail becomes permanent when one of these occurs:

1. **Player declaration** — the player naturally establishes a fact.
2. **Repeated portrayal** — play has made the trait strongly evident and the player accepts the proposed lock.
3. **Mechanical necessity** — a check requires the value now.

When mechanical necessity occurs:

- resolve the value **before** the roll;
- use established portrayal/background evidence first;
- if there is no evidence, the player may choose from the remaining legal pool;
- once the roll happens, the newly established value is locked;
- no retroactive changing because the result was inconvenient.

## Example: attributes

The character begins with:

```
Intellect: null
Perception: null
Presence: null
Resolve: null
Physical: null
remaining: [2, 2, 1, 1, 1]
```

If the first meaningful attribute question is whether the Ensign is unusually observant, the player may establish **Perception 2**. The remaining pool becomes `[2,1,1,1]`.

The other attributes remain genuinely unresolved.

## Example: Academy training

The department skill is already rank 2.

Three other broad skills may eventually be established at rank 1.

If play reveals that the Ensign took extensive shuttle electives, `Conn / Flight Control 1` can be locked and one training slot is consumed.

A skill not yet resolved is not automatically untrained.

## Example: specialty

If an Engineering Ensign repeatedly gravitates toward EPS failures, later says they concentrated on power systems at the Academy, or reaches a check where that expertise matters, the department specialty may lock as:

`Engineering → Power Distribution / EPS`

That is preferable to choosing "Warp Propulsion" simply because it sounded useful before the character existed.

## Organic background

Questions asked in ordinary play can create canon.

If an NPC asks:

> "Where did you do your Academy field placement?"

the player's answer may become a persistent background fact.

If a crewmate asks what the player does off duty, that can establish a hobby.

If family becomes relevant six months into the campaign, family details can be established then rather than front-loaded now.

Once a fact materially affects play, it must be persisted at the next checkpoint.

## Species

Any canon species plausibly serving in Starfleet in 2372 can be proposed.

Species never forces a personality stereotype.

Species/cultural/biological details are only applied when they are appropriate to the individual and established in play or canon.

## What r00001 means

Revision 1 / `r00001` represents the exact moment immediately after the new Ensign materializes in Transporter Room 2 at 1217 hours.

At that checkpoint:

- identity and department are established;
- the primary department skill is established;
- unresolved mechanical pools are persisted;
- basic Starfleet qualifications and issued equipment are present;
- orientation and department-reporting obligations exist;
- no narrative action has occurred yet.

The character continues being created through play after Scene One begins.
